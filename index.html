      How to play

      The chart shows one currency's whole history: what one unit was worth in US dollars, from 1950 or the day it was born, to today.

      Two key moments are marked on the chart from the start. You have seven guesses: each wrong guess gives you a clue about how the currency trades and marks one more moment. The first clue shades the chart blue to show how it was managed: fixed, managed or floating.

      Each guess also says whether you picked the right part of the world.

      One currency a day, the same for everyone.
